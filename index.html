🕰️ Before your visit

Check official visitor information before travelling. This concept section is designed to show where confirmed opening hours, admission information, booking requirements, and special notices could appear.

  • Confirm operating hours and admission details from authorised sources.
  • Check whether advance booking or group arrangements are required.
  • Review any temporary visitor notices before arrival.

👕 Visitor etiquette

A digital guide could provide respectful visitor guidance, including conduct, dress expectations, and any special instructions for particular spaces or ceremonies.

  • Follow instructions from staff or authorised guides.
  • Respect restricted areas and cultural protocols.
  • Ask before photographing people or ceremonial activities.
GUIDANCE SUBJECT TO AUTHORISATION
📷 Photography & recording

Photography, video, and audio recording rules may differ by location or exhibition. This prototype intentionally does not state an official policy.

  • Look for posted notices and follow staff directions.
  • Confirm restrictions before recording or sharing images.
  • Respect privacy and cultural sensitivities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Visitor FAQs

Sample questions demonstrate the FAQ experience.

Is this an official palace website?

No. This is a concept demonstration created by GatherLink Heritage. It is not an official website, endorsement or partnership.

Do I need to download an app?

The concept opens in a mobile browser from a QR code or shared link, without requiring an app.

Where can I find official visitor details?

Official details should be obtained directly from the institution or its authorised channels. This prototype does not publish verified operating information.

Can QR codes link to different information?

In a deployed system, separate QR codes could lead to approved pages for visitor information, exhibits, events or designated information points.
